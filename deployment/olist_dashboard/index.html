Metric definitions

Dates

Only events dated before the selected date are included; the selected date itself is excluded.

Cohorts

Orders and item value use purchase dates; deliveries, late share, late days, reviews and hotspots use customer delivery dates.

Overdue, not yet delivered

Orders due in the last 30 days that hadn't reached the customer by the selected date. Cancelled orders are included, because the data doesn't say when they were cancelled. 'With carrier' means handed to the carrier before the selected date; the rest are not yet shipped. The change compares with the same count 30 days earlier.

Late deliveries

A delivery is late when its customer delivery date is after the promised date. Late share plus on-time share equals 100%; average days late covers late deliveries only.

Reviews

Review averages count only reviews whose creation date and answer date are both before the selected date.

Limits

Total item value sums item prices excluding freight; it is not revenue.

How these lists are built

Parcel ranking

Open parcels are ranked each morning by two equally weighted percentiles: late-risk score and remaining slack at the 09:00 review (the smaller of slack at handover and days left before the promise). Act today is the top 10% of yesterday's handovers, rounded up; Watch is the next block of the same size; parcels past their promise go to Update customer. A parcel picked earlier is not listed again.

Why a parcel ranks

Each parcel shows its predicted and promised dates, remaining slack, late-risk percentile against training parcels, and the inputs raising its risk.

Promise dates

Our delivery-time estimate plus a regional buffer, reset each month, aiming for 3.5% late. The result line shows the outcome once enough orders are due.

Live performance

Each line is recomputed for the selected date from outcomes known by then.

    How Ask works

    Model proposes, server checks

    The provider sees the local SQLite schema and proposes one SELECT or WITH statement. The server rejects writes, multiple statements, risky functions and any query that misses the :as_of cutoff.

    Read-only and bounded

    SQLite opens read-only under a deny-by-default authorizer. Queries have a two-second deadline, return at most 200 rows and 30 columns, and identifiers, addresses and review text are blocked.

    Grounded answer

    The model answers only from the returned rows; the server rejects numbers not in the result. Item price is not revenue; association is not causation.

    Evidence

    Evidence shows the data date and, for data answers, the SQL that ran. Saved chats replay it without asking the provider again.
